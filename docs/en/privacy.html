Privacy Policy

Splits (インターバル) — 6 September 2026

Splits never connects to the internet. There are no accounts. Nothing leaves your Apple Watch, and the developer cannot see any of it.

Information collected

None. No account, no email address. No analytics, no advertising measurement, no in-app purchases.

The app contains no networking code at all.

What it reads from Health

While the timer is running, and only with your permission, it reads:

  • Heart rate
  • Active energy

These are used only to record the workout. They are never shown on screen, exported, or sent anywhere.

What it writes to Health

When the timer finishes, the session is saved to Health as a workout: start and end times, the activity type (baseball), that it was outdoors, and the heart rate and active energy above.

There is a reason for saving it. watchOS keeps an app running while a workout session is active. Without that, the timer would stop the moment you lower your wrist and the screen turns off, and the taps would not fire.
If you don't allow Health access, the app still works, but timing and taps are not guaranteed while the screen is off. The app says so on the timer screen when that happens.

What is stored on your watch

Only the settings you used last (total length and number of splits), so they are ready next time. This never leaves the watch, and is deleted with the app.

Sharing with third parties

None. There is no one to share anything with.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, this page is updated and the date at the top is changed.
